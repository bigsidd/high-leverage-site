How the record is kept

  1. Sealed before the outcome. Each call is committed and timestamped, with an outside anchor, before anyone can know how it ends.
  2. Closed, never edited. A close is a new row. Nothing already written can change.
  3. Vetoes stay ticker-less. A chain that never publishes shows only its stage and date, now and later.

    The paper book

    The rule, fixed in advance: $100,000 of fake money, an equal $10,000 per call, entered at the first official close after the call is committed, held to its horizon. An outperform call holds the stock against SPY. An underperform call holds SPY against the stock. Confidence never changes the amount. Anyone can recompute it from the ledger and public prices.
