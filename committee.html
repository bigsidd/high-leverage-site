Meet the committee

Three seats, cut so the writer, the checker and the decider are never the same.

Design preview · as designed

The three model seats

  1. The analyst

    research-analyst
    Model
    Sonnet 5.5
    Writes
    Notes on flagged anomalies, the weekly memo, monthly thesis checks.
    Covers
    Four open roles until they’re hired. Its nameplate shows which.
    Can’t
    Publish, reach a credential, or see the risk officer’s context.

  2. The risk officer

    risk-officer
    Model
    Sonnet 5.5, a separate seat
    Writes
    The dissent on every memo.
    Can
    Veto. The chain goes to the No tray, ticker-less forever.
    Can’t
    Approve anything on its own, or publish.

  3. The committee

    investment-committee
    Model
    Opus 5.5
    Decides
    Publish or not, a 3, 6 or 12 month horizon, and a low, medium or high confidence bucket.
    Reads
    Never a memo without the dissent attached.
    Can’t
    Publish. Its decision waits for one human stamp.

Each seat is one tool-less model call per step. Each gets 20 to 30 test cases, run three times, with 100% required on the must and must-not checks.

The one human

Sidd, the stamp

One stamp per call, in the Sunday digest. About 20 minutes a week. The stamp is the only thing that lets a script publish. Scores and closes run by rule and need no stamp.

The machines

Scripts do everything that is arithmetic or plumbing. They are drawn as machines in the office because they aren’t people.

Open desks and the bar to hire

The bar: two clean scheduled runs, its own 20 to 30 test cases at 100% on the must and must-not checks, and a written decision record naming the independence the new seat adds.
